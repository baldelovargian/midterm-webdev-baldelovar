Our Roasting Heritage

TRADITIONAL SLOW-SMOKED SPECIFICATIONS

Infused with native lemongrass, crushed garlic, and farm-fresh wild herbs, every roll is hand-turned over glowing mahogany charcoal for over five hours. We preserve centuries-old Filipino roasting traditions to deliver crackling, golden-crisp skin and juicy, deeply seasoned meat—with zero artificial tenderizers.
