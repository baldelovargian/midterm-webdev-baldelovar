# Royen's Lechon & Grill

**Live site:** https://YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME.github.io/Royen's-lechon-glassmorphism/

## About

ROYEN'S Lechon & Grill is a single-page culinary web portal built for a traditional charcoal-roasted lechon business. It allows customers to explore house specialties, learn about our traditional slow-roasting process, and submit pre-order reservations online.

## Design System

### Palette (60-30-10)

| Role | Share | Color | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dominant | 60% | `black` | Page background (Dark Charcoal Mahogany) |
| Secondary | 30% | `white` | Glassmorphic cards, surface panels, and navigation background |
| Accent | 10% | `orange` | CTA buttons, links, active badges, and scroll progress indicator |

### Visual style

**Style chosen:** Glassmorphism (Morphism)

**Why I chose it:** Translucent glassmorphic cards with subtle backdrop blur provide a modern, premium feel while maintaining high readability against dark charcoal mahogany backdrops.

### Contrast check

Body text color vs. background color: `#fbf8f5` on `#0f0b08`  
Contrast ratio: 17.1 : 1 (needs to be at least 4.5 : 1)  
Tool used: WebAIM Contrast Checker
